OmniDeck

Delete your account and data

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This page is for the OmniDeck Android app (com.omnideck.shell), published by {{ENTITY}}. You do not need the app installed to make a request.

If you have the app installed

Open Settings → Privacy → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm, and the deletion begins immediately.

If you do not have the app installed

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed up with, with the subject Account deletion request.

Sending from your registered address is how we confirm the request is yours. If you no longer have access to that address, say so in the message and we will verify another way rather than refusing the request.

We confirm receipt within 5 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will get a confirmation when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record, email address and profile.
  • Your entitlements and licence assignments.
  • Data held by every OmniDeck feature you have used. Deletion fans out to each installed module, which erases its own storage; nothing is left behind because a module was uninstalled first.
  • Data stored on your device by the app, including its encryption keys.
  • Analytics and diagnostic records linked to your account identifier.

What is kept, and why

DataKept forWhy
Purchase, invoice and tax records As long as the law requires Financial record-keeping obligations we cannot waive
Aggregated, de-identified usage counts Indefinitely No longer linked to you or your device, and cannot be re-identified
Records of the deletion itself Up to 2 years Evidence that we honoured your request
Encrypted backups Up to 35 days Expire on the ordinary backup rotation; not restored to re-create a deleted account

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove data. In the app, Settings → Privacy lets you turn off analytics and diagnostics, reset your analytics identifier, and remove an individual feature together with everything it stored. Uninstalling a feature erases that feature's data and leaves the rest of your account intact.
