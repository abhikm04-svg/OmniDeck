This policy explains what OmniDeck collects, why, and what control you have over it. It covers the OmniDeck Android app published by {{ENTITY}} and the services behind it.

The short version

  • Analytics and diagnostics are off until you turn them on, and you choose them by purpose rather than all at once.
  • OmniDeck does not sell your personal data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • OmniDeck does not use an advertising identifier unless a feature you install declares and justifies one.
  • You can delete your account and data without installing the app.

How OmniDeck is put together

OmniDeck is a host app that loads feature modules. Modules are delivered and updated separately from the app itself, and you install only the ones you use.

This matters for privacy, because each module is isolated rather than trusted:

  • Every module declares, in a manifest that ships with it, the capabilities it needs and the categories of data it handles. The app enforces that declaration at runtime — a module asking for something it did not declare is refused, not trusted.
  • Each module gets its own storage area, its own encryption keys and its own identity. One module cannot read another module’s data or act under another module’s name.
  • The data-safety information in our store listing is generated from those module manifests rather than written by hand, so what is disclosed and what the code does cannot drift apart unnoticed.

Where a specific module handles data beyond what is described here, its own disclosure is shown in the app before you install it.

What OmniDeck collects

Diagnostics and usage data — only if you opt in

Crash reports and usage analytics are disabled by default. If you enable them, you enable them per purpose, and you can withdraw any of them at any time in Settings → Privacy. When enabled, these records carry:

  • which module produced the event, so a fault can be attributed to the feature that caused it;
  • app version, device model, OS version and locale;
  • crash and error diagnostics, including stack traces.

Events are passed through a redaction step before they leave the device, which strips values that look like personal data. Analytics identifiers are rotating and resettable, and are not your account identifier.

Account information — only if you sign in

Parts of OmniDeck work without an account. If you do sign in, sign-in happens with our identity provider in a browser tab rather than in a form inside the app, so the app never handles your password. We store the account identifier, the email address associated with it, and what you are entitled to use. Session tokens are held in hardware-backed storage on your device.

Purchases

Purchases of digital goods are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase is valid and what it entitles you to. We never receive your card or payment details.

What OmniDeck does not do

  • It does not sell or rent personal data.
  • It does not build advertising profiles, and shows no third-party advertising.
  • It does not download and run code from outside Google Play.
  • It does not collect a list of the other apps installed on your device.

Permissions

Permissions are requested by the app on behalf of a module, at the point the feature needs them, and only if that module declared the permission up front. Denying a permission disables that feature and nothing else. Sensitive permissions are avoided in the main app by design; where a feature genuinely requires one, it is delivered as a separate app you install deliberately.

How data is stored and protected

  • Data on your device is stored in a per-module area, encrypted with keys held in the Android Keystore. Keys never leave the device.
  • Traffic to our services uses HTTPS with certificate pinning.
  • Server-side data is encrypted at rest, and access is limited to what is needed to operate the service.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with providers that run the service on our behalf — hosting, crash reporting and analytics processing — and only what they need to perform that function. They may not use it for their own purposes. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Consent. Turn analytics and diagnostics on or off per purpose, at any time, in Settings → Privacy.
  • Access and correction. Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion. Delete your account and data, in the app or from this site.
  • Reset your analytics identifier at any time, in the same screen.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your personal data, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your data-protection authority. Write to us at the address below and we will honour them.

Children

OmniDeck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where that happens, transfers are made under safeguards recognised by the relevant law.
